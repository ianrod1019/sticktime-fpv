**Settings** is your account home.

<Screenshot src="/images/settings.png" alt="The settings page" caption="Settings — profile, goal, privacy, export and account sections." />

## What lives here

| Section | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Pilot profile** | Callsign — see [Your profile](/your-profile). |
| **Weekly goal** | The hour target that drives your dashboard progress bar. |
| **Privacy** | Toggle your profile private or public. |
| **Export** | Download your data as CSV or JSON — see [Exports](/exports). |
| **Danger zone** | Account deletion with a 30-day grace period — see [Account deletion](/account-deletion). |

## Saving

Profile fields save as you confirm them; a toast confirms each save. If a save fails (offline, session expired) you'll see the error and can retry — nothing is lost silently.
