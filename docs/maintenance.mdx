Gear dies from neglect, not crashes. The hanger tracks **maintenance health** so you service things before they fail in the air.

## Maintenance logs

Every gear detail page has a **maintenance log**:

- **Add an entry** with a date, a short title (e.g. *"Replaced props"*, *"Gimbal lube"*) and notes.
- Entries keep the full service history of the item — useful when selling gear, too.

## Health alerts

The hanger computes wear from **actual usage**:

| Signal | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Flight hours on an airframe | Props/motors due for inspection after hard weeks. |
| Packs cycled on a battery set | Rebalance or retire tired sets. |
| Crash counters | A spike in crashes says inspect before you fly. |
| Time since last service | Long gaps resurface as reminders. |

Items that need attention surface at the top of the hanger with a health badge, so a glance tells you what to service next.

<Tip>
Bind gear to every [session](/logging-sessions) you fly — health math runs on
usage, and unbound sessions don't count.
</Tip>

## Crash counters

Each drone carries a crash counter on its [detail page](/drones). Log every crash — it feeds:

- **Health alerts** for that airframe
- **[Failure analytics](/failure-analytics)** in your squadron
- **Cost per hour** honestly, including the parts you burn through
