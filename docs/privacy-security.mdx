## Only you can read your logbook

Every row of your data — sessions, gear, parts, batteries, settings — is protected by **row-level security** at the database level. This is not a UI trick:

- Queries run **as you**, with your signed-in identity attached to every request.
- The database only returns rows that belong to you. Another pilot's session list, gear or ledger is not just hidden — it's **unreadable by the server on your behalf**.
- The same rules apply to edits: you can only change what's yours.

## What squadrons can see

Joining a squadron shares **what you contribute** — shared gear and the sessions flown against it — with the squadron's owners and members, per the [permission matrix](/roles-permissions). Your **personal hanger and private sessions** stay private.

## Live sync doesn't leak

Realtime updates are delivered through the same row-level security: the database only broadcasts rows your account is allowed to read. Other pilots' edits to their own data never reach your browser.

## Exports are scrubbed

CSV exports replace all internal UUIDs with sequential numbers in your browser before the file is saved — platform identifiers never leave your session. See [Exports](/exports).

## The StickTime team and your data

The StickTime team can change account-level settings — your plan tier or account status — when you ask for help. Every such action is recorded in an internal audit trail.

## Your rights: export and deletion

- **Export** everything you've stored, any time — see [Exports](/exports).
- **Delete** your account with a 30-day grace period you can cancel — see [Account deletion](/account-deletion). After the grace period, your personal data is purged.

## Sessions & sign-in

- Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth — passwords are never stored in the app database, only salted hashes in the auth service.
- "Continue with Google" uses OAuth; StickTime never sees your Google password.
- Signing out invalidates your session token on the device.
