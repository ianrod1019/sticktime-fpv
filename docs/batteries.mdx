Batteries are tracked as **sets** — a LiPo or Li-Ion pack family like "CNHL 6S 1300 (x4)" — with the individual packs inside managed separately.

## Battery sets

Create a set in **Hanger → Battery Sets** with:

- **Name and brand**
- **Cell count** (3S–8S) and **connector** (XT60, XT90...)
- **Number of packs** in the set

<Screenshot src="/images/hanger-personal.png" alt="The hanger page showing the battery sets section" />

When you [log a real session](/logging-sessions) you pick a battery set and a pack count — StickTime adds those packs to the set's lifetime total automatically.

## Individual packs

Open a battery set to manage individual packs. Each pack carries:

- **IR readings** — internal resistance per cell, measured over time. Rising IR is the classic early-warning sign of a tired pack.
- **Charging voltages** — record storage/full voltages after charging to spot cells drifting out of balance.
- **Pack status** — mark packs as healthy, tired or retired so you stop flying a pack before it puffs.

<Tip>
Log IR after every few cycles, at the same resting state (e.g. storage
charge, cooled down). Consistency makes the trend line readable.
</Tip>

## Why it matters

- **Pack totals** — the ledger splits cost per pack-flown so you know what each minute of real airtime really costs.
- **Safety** — retired packs stop appearing as usable gear when logging.
- **Squadron fleets** — shared battery sets in a squadron track usage across all members. See [Shared gear](/shared-gear).
