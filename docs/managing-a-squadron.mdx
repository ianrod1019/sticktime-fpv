Squadron owners manage everything from the **manage console** — open your squadron and click **Manage**.

<Screenshot src="/images/squadron-manage.png" alt="The squadron manage console" caption="The manage console — members, roles and permissions in one place." />

## Managing members

- **Change roles** — promote a trusted pilot to **manager**, or step a manager back down.
- **Per-member switches** — toggle gear-editing and ledger/analytics access per member.
- **Assign role templates** — bundle switches into presets and assign them in one click. See [Roles & permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Entry codes

Generate a fresh **entry code** with an expiry of your choosing (a set number of days). Codes:

- Expire automatically — no cleanup to remember.
- Can be regenerated at any time; old codes stop working when they lapse.

## The money lock

Only owners and managers can edit **purchase costs** on squadron gear. This is enforced by a database trigger — even a modified client can't write money fields as a plain member. It keeps the [squadron ledger](/squadron-ledger) trustworthy.

## Leaving, transferring and deleting

These are destructive, so they demand explicit confirmations:

- **Leaving** — an owner must transfer ownership (or delete the squadron) before leaving; managers and members can just leave.
- **Deleting the squadron** — owner-only, double-confirmed. Shared gear and membership data go with it; your personal hanger and logs are untouched.

<Warning>
Deleting a squadron is permanent for the shared space. Members keep their own
sessions and personal gear — but squadron hanger entries and ledger history
do not survive.
</Warning>
