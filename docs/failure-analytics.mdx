**Failure analytics** aggregates crashes and equipment failures across a squadron, so the whole crew can learn from every broken prop.

<Screenshot src="/images/squadron-failure-analytics.png" alt="The squadron failure analytics dashboard" caption="Failure analytics — crash counts, failure hotspots and trends across the squadron." />

## What you'll see

- **Crash trends** over time — is the squad crashing more or less?
- **Failure hotspots** — which gear (or pilots) break most often.
- **Per-member and per-rig breakdowns** — correlating crashes with airframes, packs and sessions.

## Where the data comes from

Every crash you log on a [session](/logging-sessions) and every bump of a drone's [crash counter](/drones) feeds this dashboard. Pilots who log honestly make the analytics useful for everyone.

## Who can open it

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Always. |
| **Manager** | Always. |
| **Member** | Only with the analytics grant (per-member switch or role template). |

<Tip>
Failure analytics lives at its own URL inside your squadron — Squad HQ links
to it as *Failure Analytics* when your role allows.
</Tip>
