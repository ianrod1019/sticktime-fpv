The **master parts inventory** is your bench: every spare motor, ESC, frame, camera and prop — whether it's waiting for a build or already installed.

<Screenshot src="/images/parts-inventory.png" alt="The master parts inventory page" caption="Master inventory — parts on the bench and their install status." />

## Adding parts

1. Open **Gear → Inventory**.
2. Click **Add part** and choose the category — drone parts, transmitter parts, goggle parts or other parts.
3. Record the **purchase cost** — the money fields drive per-hour costs in the ledger.

## Installs

A part is either **on the bench** or **installed** in a build:

- **Install** a part onto a [drone](/drones) from the drone's detail page (or the parts page).
- **Uninstalling** returns the part to the bench — its history stays.
- Installs record the date, so you know how long a part has lived in a build.

## Where costs go

Installed parts contribute their cost to the host drone's **cost per flight hour**. Parts on the bench don't count toward any rig's cost until they're installed — they're spare stock.

<Warning>
On **free** accounts the master inventory is limited. Full parts tracking and
installs are a **Pro** feature — and while billing isn't live yet, Pro is
limited to developers and testers. See [Billing & tiers](/billing-tiers).
</Warning>
