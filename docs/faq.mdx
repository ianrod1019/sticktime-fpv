<Accordion title="Is my data private?">
  Yes. Row-level security in the database means only you can read your logs
  and gear — other pilots (and the internet) cannot. See
  [Privacy & security](/privacy-security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use StickTime for free forever?">
  Yes. Free includes logging, dashboard, personal hanger, ledger and CSV
  export. Paid tiers add squadron features and full parts tracking — and
  they're not purchasable yet. See [Billing & tiers](/billing-tiers).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do sim sessions count toward my streak?">
  Yes — any logged session counts, sim or real. The dashboard breaks your
  streak down by sim days and real days.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why should I bind gear to every session?">
  Bound sessions power rig usage charts, cost-per-flight-hour, battery pack
  totals and maintenance health. Unbound sessions still count as airtime —
  they just can't tell you anything about your gear.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my gear if I leave a squadron?">
  Your personal hanger is untouched. Gear you contributed to the squadron
  stays with the squadron; gear you own never leaves your account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can two people track the same shared pack pool?">
  Yes — that's what squadron battery sets are for. Packs accumulate across
  all members who log against the set. See [Shared gear](/shared-gear).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
  Settings → danger zone. It's a three-step confirmation with a 30-day
  grace period you can cancel any time. See [Account deletion](/account-deletion).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Something looks wrong — a chart is empty, a page is locked.">
  Start with [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) — most cases are explained
  there (usually: bind gear, or ask your squadron owner for a grant).
</Accordion>
