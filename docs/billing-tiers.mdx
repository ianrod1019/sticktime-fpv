## How gating works

Pro-gated surfaces show a lock card with an **Upgrade** button — for example the squadron ledger for members without a grant, or parts inventory limits on free accounts:

<Screenshot src="/images/ledger-access-denied.png" alt="A locked surface with the upgrade prompt" caption="Locked surfaces explain what's missing and how to get it." />

The lock is **server-enforced** — the app hides gated features, but the database refuses unauthorized access too. A tier change takes effect on your next sign-in or token refresh.

## Upgrading (when billing launches)

1. Click **Upgrade** on any locked feature.
2. The upgrade dialog explains what you unlock.
3. Checkout runs through **Stripe** — card data never touches StickTime servers.

Until then: testers get access via manual grants, and every gated page
states it plainly.
