## The tiers

Squad sits alongside Pro: the same hobbyist feature set and the same strict
non-commercial restriction, just bundled group billing for a 5–10 pilot
club or racing squad instead of one price per pilot. It unlocks nothing a
Pro pilot doesn't already have — it only changes how a group pays.

Solo Commercial and School sit between Pro/Squad and Enterprise: everything
Pro unlocks, plus the Certification & Compliance Vault, for a single
commercial pilot or a single educational institution. Enterprise gets the
same compliance stack org-wide, on top of its multi-squadron controls.
Creating a squadron is free at every tier — it's just a roster; the paid
gate is what you can *do* with it (shared gear, ledger, fleet analytics).

Everything from the Compliance Vault down is org-gated — it only exists
for an organization on a paid plan (Solo Commercial, School, or
Enterprise), which is also why Free/Pro/Squad squadrons never see it.

## How gating works

Pro-gated surfaces show a lock card with an **Upgrade** button — for example the squadron ledger for members without a grant, or parts inventory limits on free accounts:

<Screenshot src="/images/ledger-access-denied.png" alt="A locked surface with the upgrade prompt" caption="Locked surfaces explain what's missing and how to get it." />

The lock is **server-enforced** — the app hides gated features, but the database refuses unauthorized access too. A tier change takes effect on your next sign-in or token refresh.

## Upgrading (when billing launches)

1. Click **Upgrade** on any locked feature.
2. The upgrade dialog explains what you unlock.
3. Checkout runs through **Stripe** — card data never touches StickTime servers.

Until then: testers get access via manual grants, and every gated page
states it plainly.
