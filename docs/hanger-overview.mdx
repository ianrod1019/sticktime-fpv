The **Hanger** is your gear garage. Everything you fly with — or charge with, or solder with — lives here.

<Screenshot src="/images/hanger-index.png" alt="The hanger index choosing between personal and squadron gear" caption="The Hanger section — your personal gear or a squadron's shared fleet." />

## Personal vs squadron

- **Personal hanger** — gear you own, visible only to you (unless you contribute it to a squadron — see [Shared gear](/shared-gear)).
- **Squadron hanger** — the shared fleet of a squadron you belong to. Access depends on your role — see [Roles & permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Gear sections

<Screenshot src="/images/hanger-personal.png" alt="The personal hanger with sections for drones, radios, goggles, batteries and other gear" />

The **master parts inventory** (motors, ESCs, props and spares) lives alongside — see [Parts inventory](/parts-inventory):

<Screenshot src="/images/inventory-index.png" alt="The inventory section index" caption="Inventory — the master parts list behind your builds." />

| Section | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| **Drones & Quads** | Airframes — they accumulate flight time, crash counters, cell count and component wear. |
| **Controllers & Radios** | Transmitters you pick when logging sessions, with stick-end and gimbal upgrade trackers. |
| **FPV Goggles** | Video headsets, analog modules and digital HD receivers. |
| **Battery Sets** | LiPo / Li-Ion sets with pack counts, cell count and connector types. |
| **Other Equipment** | Chargers, soldering stations, tools, backpacks and field accessories. |

## Adding gear

1. Open the section that matches your gear.
2. Click **Add gear**.
3. Fill in the name, brand and any details you know — you can always edit later.

<Tip>
Bind gear to your [sessions](/logging-sessions) as you fly. That's what powers
rig usage charts, cost-per-hour and maintenance health.
</Tip>

## Gear detail pages

Click any item to open its detail page — specs, session history, maintenance log, and (for drones and batteries) deep telemetry. See:

- [Drones](/drones) — builds, part installs, crash counters
- [Batteries](/batteries) — packs, IR readings, charging voltages
- [Transmitters, goggles & other gear](/transmitters-goggles-other)
