A **squadron** is a shared space for a crew of pilots: shared gear, roster stats, failure analytics and a common ledger.

<Screenshot src="/images/squadron-portal.png" alt="The squadron portal listing your squadron" caption="The Squadron Portal — join via code or establish a new squadron." />

## Joining a squadron

You need an **entry code** from the squadron's owner or manager:

1. Open **Squadron** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Join via Code** and paste the code.
3. You're in — you stay a member until you leave.

Entry codes are **time-sensitive**: owners generate them with an expiry (a set number of days), so old codes stop working on their own.

## Creating a squadron

<Note>
Creating a squadron is a **Pro** feature. While billing isn't live yet, Pro is
limited to developers and testers — see [Billing & tiers](/billing-tiers).
</Note>

1. Open **Squadron** and click **Establish Squadron**.
2. Name it and describe it.
3. You become the **owner** — see [Roles & permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Squad HQ

Click into your squadron to open **Squad HQ**:

<Screenshot src="/images/squadron-hq.png" alt="Squad HQ with roster and metrics" caption="Squad HQ — roster, flight time and packs across the crew." />

- **Roster** — every member with their role, join date, session count, flight time and packs flown.
- **Quick links** — the shared [hanger](/shared-gear), [ledger](/squadron-ledger) and [failure analytics](/failure-analytics) (as your role allows).
- **Manage** — owners get the [manage console](/managing-a-squadron).

## Leaving a squadron

Leave any time from the squadron page — your personal data stays yours. Owners leaving or deleting a squadron is handled in the [manage console](/managing-a-squadron) with explicit confirmations.
