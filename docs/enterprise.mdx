Enterprise unlocks the top of the org chart: a **district** (parent
organization) that manages several **sub-squadrons**, database-enforced
account lockdowns, and a team meetup scheduler.

## District HQ

District admins manage the whole org chart from **District HQ**:
aggregate metrics across every sub-squadron (active pilots, combined
fleet, flight hours) and a card grid that drills into each sub-team's
hangar and roster. Metrics are aggregate-only — a district admin sees
counts, not other squads' rosters.

## Account lockdowns

Squadron and district admins can enforce compliance rules per squadron:

- **Lock profile settings** — pilots cannot edit their callsign, bio,
  or weekly goal.
- **Require pre-flight checklist** — flight logging stays locked until
  the session's checklist is complete.
- **Enforce minimum firmware** — sessions require hull firmware at or
  above the squadron minimum.
- **Lock team inventory** — pilots cannot modify the shared hangar.

The lock is **database-enforced**, not a UI hint: writes that break an
active policy are rejected by the server even outside the app. Pilots
see which enforcements are active on their squadron's **Policy status**
tab.

## Meetup scheduler

Each squadron gets an agenda of upcoming practice sessions, race days,
and build workshops. Squadron admins schedule events; members RSVP
(attending / declined). After the event, logging the flight from the
meetup card ties the session back to the meetup for streamlined team
tracking.
