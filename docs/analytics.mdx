**Analytics** turns your logbook into insight. Open it from the sidebar — you'll find a **Personal** view of your own flying.

<Screenshot src="/images/analytics-index.png" alt="The analytics section index" caption="The Analytics section — personal analytics for your own flying." />

## Personal analytics

<Screenshot src="/images/analytics-personal.png" alt="Personal analytics page with charts over your logged sessions" />

What you'll find:

| View | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Airtime trends** | How your hours are trending week over week and month over month. |
| **Sim vs real split** | Whether you're sharpening skills in the sim or at the field. |
| **Per-rig breakdown** | Which airframes earn their shelf space. |
| **Consistency** | Streaks and gaps — the same data as the dashboard heatmap, with more context. |

## Where the data comes from

Everything in analytics is computed from your [logged sessions](/logging-sessions). The more detail you log — bound gear, packs, crashes — the richer the picture.

<Info>
Squadron-level failure analytics (crashes and broken parts across a whole
squadron) lives in the squadron section — see [Failure
analytics](/failure-analytics).
</Info>
