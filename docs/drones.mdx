Every quad you own lives in **Hanger → Drones & Quads**.

## Adding a drone

Open the section, click **Add gear**, and give it a name. Useful extras:

- **Brand / model**
- **Cell count** (e.g. 6S) and **connector** (XT60, etc.)
- **Notes** — dry weight, motor size, anything

## The drone detail page

Click a drone to open its detail page. From here you can:

- **See total flight time** — every session bound to this airframe, summed automatically.
- **Track the crash counter** — increment after a crash; the number feeds [failure analytics](/failure-analytics) in squadrons.
- **Manage the build** — the list of parts currently installed on the airframe.

## Builds and part installs

<Screenshot src="/images/parts-inventory.png" alt="The parts inventory page showing drone parts and installs" caption="Parts and installs — what you have on the bench and what's in each build." />

A **build** is the set of parts installed on a drone. When a part breaks and you replace it, **install the new part** on the drone — the old one comes back to your spares automatically with its install history intact.

1. Add the part to your [parts inventory](/parts-inventory) (or buy it straight into it).
2. Open the drone's detail page and install the part.
3. The install records the date — and the part's cost joins the drone's ledger math.

<Tip>
Swapped a motor? Install the replacement instead of editing the old part.
Your build history and cost ledger stay truthful.
</Tip>

## What powers the ledger

Every part you install on a drone, and its purchase cost, flows into **cost per flight hour** for that airframe in the [personal ledger](/personal-ledger). A hangar queen costs nothing per hour — a quad you race weekly spreads its repair costs across real airtime.
