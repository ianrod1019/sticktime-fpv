Your profile is how other pilots see you in squadrons and on shared surfaces.

## Where to edit it

Open **Settings** from the sidebar. The pilot profile section covers:

<Screenshot src="/images/settings.png" alt="The settings page with callsign, goal and privacy controls" caption="Settings — callsign, weekly goal and privacy in one place." />

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Callsign** | Your pilot name — shown on squadron rosters. Up to 24 characters. |
| **Weekly goal (hours)** | Powers the goal progress bar on your dashboard. |
| **Private profile** | Hides your callsign and details from other pilots. |

Toggle **Private profile** to hide your details from other pilots. Squadron owners and managers still see your membership, but your personal stats stay hidden.

<Warning>
Even with a private profile, sessions and gear you contribute to a squadron are
visible to that squadron's owners and managers — that's how shared ledgers and
analytics work.
</Warning>

## Saving

Click **Save profile** to apply changes — or just press **Enter** in any field. A toast confirms the save, and your callsign updates across the app (dashboard greeting, squadron rosters) immediately.

## Weekly goal

Your **weekly goal** (in hours) drives the progress bar on the [dashboard](/dashboard). Set a realistic number — many pilots start at 5 hours a week.
