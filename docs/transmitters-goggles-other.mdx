Three more sections round out the hanger.

## Controllers & radios

Track your transmitters with upgrade-aware details:

- **Gimbal and stick-end tracking** — record upgrades so you know what's installed.
- **Session binding** — every [session](/logging-sessions) you log can reference the radio you flew with.
- **Cost** — the purchase price flows into the [ledger](/personal-ledger) spread over the hours you've flown with it.

## FPV goggles

Log your headsets — analog or digital — with:

- **Receiver type** — analog module, HD receiver, or both.
- **Session binding and cost tracking**, same as radios.
- **Maintenance logs** — fan replacements, lens work, firmware notes.

## Other equipment

Chargers, soldering irons, tools, backpacks — anything that isn't a quad, radio, goggles or battery. These support:

- **Maintenance logs** for service history.
- **Cost tracking** in the ledger.

## Detail pages

Every item opens a detail page with:

- Specs and purchase info (cost, date)
- Session history where the gear was used
- The full maintenance log
- Edit and delete controls

<Info>
Gear you delete leaves your stats — sessions keep working but lose the
reference. Prefer editing a name or marking gear retired over deleting it.
</Info>
