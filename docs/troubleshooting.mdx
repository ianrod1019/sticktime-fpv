## Empty charts on the dashboard

**Charts (rig usage, pack totals, sim/real donut) are empty even though I logged sessions.**

Charts run on **bound gear**. Sessions without a bound drone can't appear in rig usage; real sessions without a battery set don't move pack totals.

*Fix:* edit your recent sessions and bind the gear. See [Logging sessions](/logging-sessions).

## A squadron page says I don't have access

**The squadron ledger or analytics shows a lock.**

Ledger and analytics are **granted**, not automatic, for members. Owners and managers always have access.

*Fix:* ask your squadron owner to grant you ledger access (per-member switch or role template). See [Roles & permissions](/roles-permissions).

## I can't create a squadron

**The Establish Squadron button is gated.**

Squadron creation is a **Pro** feature. Paid tiers are currently limited to developers and testers while billing isn't live — see [Billing & tiers](/billing-tiers).

## My gear list is empty in the log dialog

**The log session dialog has no drones/radios/goggles to pick.**

The dialog lists gear from **your personal hanger** (plus usable squadron gear). If the hanger is empty, the pickers are too.

*Fix:* add gear in the [Hanger](/hanger-overview) first.

## Changes on my phone don't show on my desktop

**Another device's edits aren't appearing.**

Screens live-update through realtime sync. If a page sat open through a network blip, refresh once — edits are safe in the database; the page just missed the event.

## My battery set's pack count is wrong

**Packs flown went to the wrong set, or the count is off.**

Pack counts come from the **battery set chosen when logging** a real session.

*Fix:* edit the session and correct the set or the packs-flown number. See [Batteries](/batteries).

## I forgot my password

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in modal. Password-reset emails go to **real inboxes only** — if your account email is a fake or dead address, password recovery isn't possible; contact the StickTime team.
