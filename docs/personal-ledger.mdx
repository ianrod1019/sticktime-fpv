The **Cost Ledger** answers the question every pilot avoids: *what does this hobby actually cost me per hour?*

## How it's calculated

For every item in your hanger:

```
cost per flight hour = (purchase price + installed parts + repairs)
                       ÷ flight hours bound to that item
```

- **Purchase costs** come from the price you record on gear and parts.
- **Hours** come from [sessions](/logging-sessions) bound to that gear.
- Unflown gear shows **cost so far, zero hours** — the hangar-queen warning.

## Views

The ledger has two shareable views, switchable with the tab control (or `?tab=breakdown`):

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Fleet overview** | Total spend, total hours, blended cost per hour across everything. |
| **Per-gear breakdown** | Cost per hour for each item — sorted so the expensive toys are obvious. |

## Pack totals

Battery costs spread across packs flown, so you see the per-pack cost of real-world airtime — a number that surprises most pilots the first time.

<Tip>
The ledger only knows what you tell it. Add purchase prices when you add
gear — retrofit them later from each item's detail page.
</Tip>

## From the ledger to the hanger

Click through any row to the gear's detail page to fix a price, add a part or review its session history. Adding a purchase from the ledger deep-links straight into the hanger's add-gear dialog.
