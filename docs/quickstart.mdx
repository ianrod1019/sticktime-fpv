From zero to your first logged session in five minutes.

## 1. Create your account

Open [StickTime FPV](https://sticktime-fpv.lovable.app) and click **Start your logbook**.

<Screenshot src="/images/sign-in.png" alt="The StickTime FPV sign-in modal" caption="Sign in with Google or an email and password." />

You can continue with **Google** or use an **email and password**.

<Screenshot src="/images/create-account.png" alt="The create account modal with email and password fields" caption="Creating an account — Google or email and password." />

If you signed up with email, confirm your address through the link we send you, then sign in.

<Tip>
Signed up with Google and want a password later? Use *Forgot password* on the
sign-in modal with your Google email to set one.
</Tip>

## 2. Meet your dashboard

After signing in you land on the **Dashboard** — your airtime at a glance. It starts empty and fills in as you log.

<Screenshot src="/images/dashboard.png" alt="An empty dashboard with total airtime, streak, packs flown and active rigs cards" />

## 3. Add gear to your hanger

Before logging, add at least one rig so sessions can reference it:

1. Open **Hanger** from the sidebar.
2. Pick the section that matches your gear — drones, radios, goggles, batteries or other.
3. Click **Add gear**, give it a name (e.g. *"Source One 5\""*) and save.

<Screenshot src="/images/hanger-personal.png" alt="The personal hanger with gear sections" />

Full details live in [Hanger overview](/hanger-overview).

## 4. Log your first session

1. Open **Log** from the sidebar.
2. Choose the **Real** or **Sim** tab.
3. Click **Log a session**, pick your duration in 5-minute blocks, bind your gear, and save.

<Screenshot src="/images/log-session-dialog.png" alt="The log session dialog with duration, gear pickers and notes" />

That's it — your hours now flow into the dashboard, heatmap and ledger.

## 5. Make it yours

Set your callsign, weekly hour goal and accent color in [Your profile](/your-profile).
