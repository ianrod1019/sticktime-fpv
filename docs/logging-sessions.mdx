The **Log** page is your flight logbook. It has two timecards that stay separate:

- **Real** — real-world quads and packs flown
- **Sim** — simulator runs (VelociDrone, Liftoff, and more)

## Log a session

1. Open **Log** and pick the **Real** or **Sim** tab.
2. Click **Log a session** (or **Log sim session** on the Sim tab).
3. Fill in the dialog:

<Screenshot src="/images/log-session-dialog.png" alt="The log session dialog with date, duration blocks, gear pickers and notes" />

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Date flown** | Defaults to today. |
| **Duration** | Chosen in 5-minute blocks. |
| **Drone / Transmitter / Goggles** | Pick from gear in your hanger. Everything is optional but the more you bind, the better your rig stats and ledger get. |
| **Battery set** (Real only) | Attributes the session's packs to a battery set for pack totals and health. |
| **Sim platform** (Sim only) | VelociDrone, Liftoff, and more. |
| **Packs flown** (Real only) | How many packs you went through. |
| **Crashes** | Anything you walked away from counts. |
| **Notes** | Free-form. Weather, track, who you flew with — anything. |

4. Click **Save**. The session appears in your log instantly and your dashboard updates.

<Tip>
Switch tabs with the **Real / Sim** toggle at the top — or deep-link with
`?tab=sim` / `?tab=real` in the URL.
</Tip>

## Editing and deleting sessions

Every session in the list can be edited or deleted. Deletion asks for confirmation and immediately frees the hours back out of your stats.

## How sessions feed everything else

- **[Dashboard](/dashboard)** — total airtime, streak, heatmap, monthly volume and rig usage.
- **[Cost ledger](/personal-ledger)** — sessions bound to gear drive cost-per-flight-hour.
- **Battery health** — packs flown update pack counts and health on your battery sets ([Batteries](/batteries)).
- **Squadron HQ** — sessions you fly count toward your squadron's roster stats ([Squadrons](/squadron-overview)).

<Warning>
Sim sessions never consume battery packs and real sessions never use a sim
platform — the timecards keep the two worlds clean so your analytics stay
honest.
</Warning>
