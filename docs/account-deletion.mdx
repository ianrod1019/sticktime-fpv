Decided to hang up the goggles? Deletion is designed to be deliberate — and reversible for 30 days.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/settings.png" alt="The settings page danger zone" />

1. **Confirm your intent** — type *delete my account*.
2. **Acknowledge finality** — type *this is not reversible*.
3. **Double-check** — two explicit checkboxes confirming you understand.

Only then is deletion **scheduled** — nothing is purged immediately.

## The 30-day grace period

- Deletion executes on the **scheduled date**, 30 days out.
- The Settings page shows a **pending-deletion card** with the exact date.
- **Cancel any time** during the window — one click and your account is fully restored to normal. No data was lost, because nothing was deleted yet.

## What gets deleted

When the grace period ends:

- Your profile, sessions, gear, parts, batteries and maintenance history.
- Personal data is scrubbed from squadron rosters you belonged to.

<Tip>
Export everything first — see [Exports](/exports). Once the grace period
ends, the data is gone for good.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Squadrons you **own** need attention before deletion: transfer ownership or
delete the squadron, or the squadron is left without an owner.
</Warning>
