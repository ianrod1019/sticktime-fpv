Your dashboard is the at-a-glance home for all your airtime.

<Screenshot src="/images/dashboard.png" alt="The StickTime FPV dashboard with quick stats, consistency grid and charts" />

## Quick stats

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total airtime** | Every logged minute across sim and real. |
| **Current streak** | Consecutive days with at least one session. The subtitle splits sim vs real streaks. |
| **Packs flown** | Real-world packs logged — your LiPo wear indicator. |
| **Active rigs** | Unique drones flown in the last 30 days. |

## Quick Log

Need to log without leaving the dashboard? **Quick Log** opens the same session dialog from the [Log page](/logging-sessions).

## Consistency grid

A GitHub-style heatmap of the **last 12 months**. Every square is a day; darker means more airtime that day. It's the fastest way to spot gaps — and streaks.

## Airtime consolidated view

A donut chart splitting **Sim vs Real** hours with percentages, so you can see whether your stick time skews to the field or the simulator.

## Monthly volume

Stacked bars of hours per month over the last 12 months, with sim and real stacked separately. Spot seasonal dips (winter...) at a glance.

## Rig usage

Horizontal bars showing hours per drone. Sessions without a bound drone can't appear here — another reason to bind gear when you log.

<Tip>
The dashboard refreshes automatically. Edit a log on your phone and your
desktop updates without a reload — edits from any device or tab appear live.
</Tip>

## Goal progress

Your [weekly goal](/your-profile) renders as a progress bar — fill it to keep the streak alive.
